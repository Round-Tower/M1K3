# Weekend loaf — formula (demo document)

Baker's percentages, for a single 900 g loaf.

| Ingredient | % | grams |
|---|---|---|
| Strong white flour | 90 | 450 |
| Wholemeal flour | 10 | 50 |
| Water | 72 | 360 |
| Levain (100% hydration) | 20 | 100 |
| Salt | 2 | 10 |

Autolyse 45 min. Four sets of folds over two hours. Bulk to ~60% rise, shape,
cold-retard 12–14 h. Bake at 240 °C, 20 min lidded, 22 min uncovered.
