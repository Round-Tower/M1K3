Thanks for testing M1K3 1.3.0 on the Mac.

New in this build, please try:
• Talk over me: in voice mode, start speaking while I'm answering and I should stop and listen. Use the Mac's own mic or a USB one; Bluetooth headsets keep the old behaviour. Tell us about any time I stopped when nobody spoke, or kept talking through you
• Let a long answer play out in voice mode: sources are no longer read aloud
• Have a longer chat, ten messages or more, on Lil or Big: replies from the third message on should start sooner than in 1.2.1. Tell us your Mac model if it feels slower
• Drop a manual or report onto the chat and ask for a number or name that's in it (a torque, a date, who signed off): the answer should come from your document, not a general web answer

And the basics: chat on Mini, attach an image for Lil, drop a PDF onto the chat, and the m1k3 command-line tool with your coding agent. Until Nov 1 the Fox should be in its Halloween look.

The network is used for brain downloads and web search, which is on by default — one switch in Settings turns it off. Flag anything that looks like it left your Mac when it shouldn't have: Settings → "Report an issue…" attaches a privacy-scrubbed diagnostic.
