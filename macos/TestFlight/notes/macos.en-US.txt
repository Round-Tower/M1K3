Thanks for testing M1K3 1.2.1 on the Mac.

New in this build, please try:
• Leave the chat window in front, composer empty, and touch nothing for about 90 seconds: the companion (or pixel face) behind the chat should fade out
• Then hover over the window, click, type or scroll: it should fade straight back in
• Type a draft and walk away: it should stay awake while there's text in the box
• Ask something long, by voice or text, and don't touch anything: it should stay awake while I listen, think, answer and speak. The 90 s counts from your last input, so it may rest soon after I finish; tell us if that feels abrupt
• While it's asleep, start voice mode: it should come back as I start listening
• Same in the menu-bar popover and the brain picker
• Turn on Reduce Motion (System Settings → Accessibility → Display): it should vanish and return instantly, no fade
• With it asleep, open Activity Monitor: M1K3's CPU should drop to near zero. Tell us your Mac model and both numbers, awake and asleep
• On a fresh install, the face should stay awake through first-run setup while the brain downloads

And the basics: chat on Mini, attach an image for Lil, voice mode, drop a PDF onto the chat, and the m1k3 command-line tool with your coding agent. From Oct 24 to Nov 1 the Fox should be in its Halloween look.

The network is used for brain downloads and web search, which is on by default — one switch in Settings turns it off. Flag anything that looks like it left your Mac when it shouldn't have: Settings → "Report an issue…" attaches a privacy-scrubbed diagnostic.
