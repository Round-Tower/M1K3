Thanks for testing M1K3 1.3.0 on the Mac.

New in this build, please try:
• Start a new chat and ask for a recipe. The answer should be the recipe, with no sentence about what leaves your Mac before it. Then ask "does anything I type ever leave this Mac?": the answer should still say yes, web searches do
• Web search switch: it sits beside the brain switcher in the chat toolbar. Turn it off and ask for today's news: I should answer without going to the web
• Tell me how you like answers: say "Keep your answers to three sentences or fewer, please." Quit, reopen and ask an open question. The answer should stay short. The ask shows in Memories, where you can delete it
• Answers take the shape of what you asked: a how-to as numbered steps, a comparison side by side, small talk as plain chat. Tell us when a shape feels wrong
• Thumbs down an answer, then "Send this exchange…": your mail app opens with that one exchange, shown first so you can edit it. Nothing is sent unless you press Send
• Drop a scanned PDF (photos of pages) onto the chat: I read it on your Mac and say how many pages I read. Before, it said "no readable text"
• Ask "are you ready?" while a brain downloads: I should say how far along it is and who answers meanwhile
• On a hot Mac, or in Low Power Mode, I say once why I'm taking it easier
• If M1K3 ever quits unexpectedly, the next launch shows a short note offering a report. You see the report before anything is sent, and no rating request comes for 3 days

Also new since 1.2.1:
• Talk over me: in voice mode, start speaking while I'm answering and I should stop and listen. Use the Mac's own mic or a USB one; Bluetooth headsets keep the old behaviour. Tell us about any time I stopped when nobody spoke, or kept talking through you
• Sources are no longer read aloud in voice mode
• Long chats on Lil or Big: replies from the third message on should start sooner than in 1.2.1. Tell us your Mac model if it feels slower
• Drop a manual or report onto the chat and ask for a number or name that's in it: the answer should come from your document

The network is used for brain downloads and web search, which is on by default; the switch above turns it off. Flag anything that looks like it left your Mac when it shouldn't have: Settings → "Report an issue…" attaches a privacy-scrubbed diagnostic.
