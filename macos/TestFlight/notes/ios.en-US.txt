Thanks for testing M1K3 1.4.0 on iPhone and iPad.

New in this build, please try:
• The eye at the top of a chat opens the camera. Point it at a label, a sign or a screen, type a question and send. The answer should be about what you see, and come from your device: try it in Airplane Mode
• Ask again: after a look, tap "Ask about this photo" to ask a second question about the same picture
• Search: after a look, tap "Search the web". A search should appear in the message field and go nowhere until you send it
• Voice mode with the eye open: every question should be about what is in front of you. Ask something that has nothing to do with the view ("what's seven times eight?") and I should just answer it
• Voice mode with the eye closed: ask "what am I looking at?" and I should take one look and close the camera. Settings has a switch, "Open the camera when I ask aloud": turn it off and the same question should open nothing
• The green camera dot should go out when you leave the Look screen, leave voice mode or put the app in the background. Tell us about any time it stays on
• A long look: keep the eye open in voice mode for about ten minutes. Tell us if the phone gets hot, and how much battery it took
• Delete a chat that has a look in it: the picture should go with it
• The title on the chat screen names who is answering, like "LIL M1K3"
• Ask Mini an ordinary coding question ("write a function that reverses a string"): the answer should be code, with no line about not sharing its wiring

And the basics:
• Voice mode, a few turns in a row
• Airplane mode: chat should keep working on the device
• Brain at Home with M1K3 on your Mac

The camera picture is read on your device and sent nowhere. The network is used for brain downloads and web search, which is on by default. Send feedback straight from TestFlight; a screenshot of anything odd helps most.
