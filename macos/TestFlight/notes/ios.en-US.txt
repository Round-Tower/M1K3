Thanks for testing M1K3 1.2 on iPhone and iPad.

New in this build, please try:
• Start a Lil download (8 GB devices), then leave the app: it should keep going, with progress on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Turn on Settings → Speak answers: each reply should be read aloud as it arrives. Sound effects turns the small sounds off
• On a fresh install, setup's face card should reveal your companion
• Ask Lil something that takes thought: the answer should follow its thinking straight after
• Ask what you decided or told it earlier: it should answer from your memory, not the web
• Turn memory capture off, chat, relaunch: nothing from that chat should appear in memory

And the basics: chat on Mini, voice mode, a PDF under Documents, airplane mode, and Brain at Home with M1K3 on your Mac.

The network is used for brain downloads and web search, which is on by default — one switch in Settings turns it off. Send feedback straight from TestFlight; a screenshot of anything odd helps most.
