Thanks for testing M1K3 1.3.0 on iPhone and iPad.

New in this build, please try:
• Start a new chat and ask for a recipe ("get me a recipe for Bolognese"). The answer should be the recipe: no "I don't share my wiring" and no sentence about DuckDuckGo before it. Then ask "does anything I type ever leave this device?": the answer should still say yes, web searches do
• The line over the chat field says who answers and whether web search is on, like "Lil · Web on". Tap it to switch brains, or to turn web search off for the next question
• One ＋ beside the field holds Files and Photos, plus the same brain and web switches
• On a folding iPhone, try the chat and Settings both open and closed
• Tell me how you like answers: say "Keep your answers short, please." Close the app, reopen it and ask an open question. The answer should stay short
• Reply "yes", "go on" or a thumbs-up emoji to an offer: I should carry on, not repeat my last message
• Ask Lil "what's in tech news?": it should search the web, and not repeat an earlier answer
• Downloads: before a brain downloads I check there's room, and the bar moves the whole way. If one fails, I say why, and "Send a report" appears under it
• Add a scanned PDF under Documents: I read it on your device and say how many pages I read
• Ask "are you ready?" while a brain downloads: I should say how far along it is
• Thumbs down an answer and type a note: the note box should stay open while you type
• If M1K3 ever quits unexpectedly, the next launch shows a short note offering a report. You see the report before anything is sent, and no rating request comes for 3 days

And the basics:
• Voice mode, a few turns in a row. Sources are no longer read aloud
• Airplane mode: chat should keep working on the device
• Brain at Home with M1K3 on your Mac

The network is used for brain downloads and web search, which is on by default. Send feedback straight from TestFlight; a screenshot of anything odd helps most.
