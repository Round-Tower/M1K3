## Paste into Resolution Center:

Thank you for the review. The app does include Age Assurance via Apple's Declared Age Range API. We apologise — our review notes gave the wrong navigation path for iPhone/iPad.

**Where to find it on iPhone/iPad:**
Settings (gear icon, top-right of the chat screen) > scroll to "Content Controls" section > tap "Set up"

This presents Apple's system Declared Age Range sheet (ageGates: 13, 16, 18). The effects:
- Under 16: web search and web page tools are removed from the AI agent's tool palette (code enforcement, not prompt-only)
- All minor bands: the assistant's tone adjusts to an age-appropriate, encouraging register
- Declining gives full capability — undeclared is identical to adult

Our previous review notes said "Settings > Privacy > Content Controls" — that is the Mac path. On iPhone/iPad, the Content Controls section sits directly in the Settings form (there is no "Privacy" tab on mobile). We have updated the review notes to include both paths.

Thank you.
