NEW IN 1.5

• I speak your language. The app is now in German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese as well as English, following your Mac's language, and I reply in the language you write in, whichever brain is answering. In voice mode I still listen in English for now; an answer in another language is read by a voice that speaks it. A few privacy notes stay in English until a native speaker has checked them.
• Read a text. Drop a .txt, .md, .csv or .log file on the chat and ask about it ("when does the warranty end?"). The answer sticks to the file, and the file goes nowhere else. Ask more keeps asking about the same text; Keep this saves it to your documents.
• Lil works it out. On a multi-step problem, a sum or a puzzle, Lil now works through it in short steps before answering: more right answers, and still the fast brain.
• I tell you when I'm thinking. When a question needs real thought, I say so in one plain line, instead of leaving you with a spinner.
• See what I'm quoting. In voice mode, when I quote a web page, click the chip or say "show me that" and the page opens beside me with the quote lit up. "Scroll down", "go back" and "close it" work too. A page never opens by itself.
• I say numbers the way people do: dates, times, money, fractions, versions and "Henry VIII". And "I'm", "I'll" and "I've" sound right now.
• Plainer talk. The villain is the look, not the voice. Ask me to talk plainer and I will.
• Accessibility: every control has a name for VoiceOver and Voice Control, and the contrast is measured.
• A clean start: a new chat opens without suggested questions.
• Fixed: an answer could stop at certain emoji.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Three brains: Mini (instant, on Apple Intelligence), Lil (4B, sees images) and Big (12B). Pick the one that suits your Mac.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: drop in notes, PDFs and text files and I answer from them, on your Mac.
• I remember what matters to you, in a memory you can see, search and delete.
• Call memory: with your consent, I transcribe and summarise calls, encrypted on your Mac.
• Brain at Home: pair your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi and use your Mac's brain from the sofa.
• Your coding agents can use my knowledge and voice through a local MCP server and the m1k3 command-line tool.
• Your other apps can use my brain through the Local API, on this Mac only.
• Siri and Shortcuts can ask me things and add to what I know.
• Private Cloud Compute, if you want it: off by default, one message at a time, with your consent.

Everything else runs on your Mac. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for model downloads, web search and the optional private cloud. Settings > Brains & downloads names every download; web search and the private cloud are switches in Settings > Privacy, and lending the private cloud to other apps is its own switch in Settings > Server.
