NEW IN 1.6

• Dressed for the season. From 2 December I glow candy red and gold for Christmas and New Year, and through the year I dress up for Lunar New Year, Ramadan and Eid, St Patrick's Day and Nowruz. One switch in Settings turns the looks off.
• Code beside the chat. Ask me for a small web page and it opens beside the chat, ready to look at, not just read. Click Open on any code block to see that one. Pages are sealed: no scripts run and nothing loads from the internet. Share or save a page as an .html file.
• Open With M1K3. Right-click a text, code or JSON file in Finder and choose Open With ▸ M1K3, or drop it on me in the Dock. It lands in the box, ready to ask about, and never sends by itself.
• If a file arrives while I'm answering, I tell you it's waiting and put it in the box when I'm done.

IN CASE YOU MISSED IT (1.4 AND 1.5)

• I speak your language: the app is in German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese as well as English, and I reply in the language you write in.
• Read a text. Drop a .txt, .md, .csv or .log file on the chat and ask about it. The file goes nowhere else.
• Lil works through a multi-step problem in short steps, and I tell you in one plain line when a question needs real thought.
• In voice mode, the web page I quote opens beside me with the quote lit up.
• I say dates, money and numbers the way people do. Ask me to talk plainer and I will.
• Every control has a name for VoiceOver and Voice Control.
• The Local API: switch it on in Settings > Server and any app that speaks the OpenAI API can use the brain you have loaded, on this Mac only. Off by default, with its own key.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Three brains: Mini (instant, on Apple Intelligence), Lil (4B, sees images) and Big (12B). Pick the one that suits your Mac.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: drop in notes, PDFs and text files and I answer from them, on your Mac.
• I remember what matters to you, in a memory you can see, search and delete.
• Call memory: with your consent, I transcribe and summarise calls on your Mac. The saved call record is encrypted.
• Brain at Home: pair your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi and use your Mac's brain from the sofa.
• Your coding agents can use my knowledge and voice through a local MCP server and the m1k3 command-line tool.
• Your other apps can use my brain through the Local API, on this Mac only.
• Siri and Shortcuts can ask me things and add to what I know.
• Private Cloud Compute, if you want it: off by default. Pick it as a brain and I ask the first time.

Everything else runs on your Mac. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for model downloads, web search and the optional private cloud. Settings > Brains & downloads names every download; web search and the private cloud are switches in Settings > Privacy, and lending the private cloud to other apps is its own switch in Settings > Server.
