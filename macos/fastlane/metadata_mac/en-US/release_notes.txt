NEW IN 1.1

• A new welcome. Setup is a short deck on glass, with pixel titles, a few small sounds and a word from me when you're done. Its last card, My full kit, gets Lil, semantic memory, sharper ears and my voice in one tap while you carry on. Re-run it any time from Settings > General.
• Brains & downloads. One Settings page lists everything I can download, with its size, live progress, Cancel and Resume, and Remove to give you the space back. A download that times out now retries on its own.
• Lil can see. Lil is now Qwen3.5 (4B) and it reads images: attach a photo, a screenshot or a whiteboard and ask about it. It downloads once more (about 3 GB); remove the old Lil in Settings > Brains & downloads. The Reasoning setting works for Lil again.
• Semantic memory rides with Lil. Pick it with Lil and I switch your search over to it as soon as Lil is ready.
• Remember this photo. Under any image you send, one tap keeps a private caption of it in my memory, so you can ask about it later. It never leaves your Mac, agents connected over MCP can't read it, and deleting the chat forgets it.
• Meet the Phosphor Fox, the new default companion: a white lattice. If you already picked one, it stays yours.
• A tidier Settings. MCP Server and Brain at Home have their own pages. Model auditions and weight import are gone: if you imported audition weights, they're deleted once to give you the space back.
• Your companion, behind the chat. The avatar now sits in the window's background, or switch it off, with no side panel. The thinking rain is gone for now. VoiceOver announces when I start thinking, listening or speaking.
• Halloween in the Lair. From October 24 to November 1, I dress up. Turn it off in Settings > M1K3 > Companion > Seasonal looks.
• Faster Big. Big (Gemma 4) no longer spends about a second per step re-reading its own prompt.
• Quicker tools on every brain, a fix for a tool call Lil sometimes wrote with a stray tag, and a rejected tool call now tells the brain why so it can try again. Chat titles no longer show my thinking.
• No more stalls when the display sleeps. One call that could not be read no longer hides the rest of your call list.
• If you turned on MLX semantic embeddings before, I rebuild your search index once, the next time your Mac is idle and cool.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Three brains: Mini (instant, on Apple Intelligence), Lil (4B, sees images) and Big (12B). Pick the one that fits your Mac.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: drop in notes and PDFs and I answer from them, on your Mac.
• I remember what matters to you, in a memory you can see, search and delete.
• Call memory: with your consent, I transcribe and summarise calls, encrypted on your Mac.
• Brain at Home: pair your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi and use your Mac's brain from the couch.
• Your coding agents (Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Codex, Zed) can use my knowledge and voice through a local MCP server and the m1k3 command-line tool.
• Siri and Shortcuts can ask me things and add to what I know.
• Private Cloud Compute, if you want it: off by default, one message at a time, with your consent.

Everything else runs on your Mac. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for model downloads, web search and the optional private cloud. Settings > Brains & downloads names every download; web search and the private cloud are switches in Settings > Privacy.
