NEW IN 1.3

• Talk over me. In voice mode, start speaking while I'm answering and I stop and listen. Echo cancellation is now on by default, so I hear you and not myself. It works with your Mac's own mic or a USB one; Bluetooth headsets keep the old behavior.
• Answers, not links. When I speak a reply aloud, I stop at the sources instead of reading out every web address.
• Quicker in long chats. I pick up where the last message left off instead of rereading the whole conversation. Measured on an M1 Max, past the tenth message: Big gets to its answer about six seconds sooner, Lil nearly a second.
• Your documents first. Ask for a number or a name that's in a manual or report you've added, like a torque, a date or who signed off, and I answer from your document, on your Mac, instead of looking it up online.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Three brains: Mini (instant, on Apple Intelligence), Lil (4B, sees images) and Big (12B). Pick the one that fits your Mac.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: drop in notes and PDFs and I answer from them, on your Mac.
• I remember what matters to you, in a memory you can see, search and delete.
• Call memory: with your consent, I transcribe and summarise calls, encrypted on your Mac.
• Brain at Home: pair your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi and use your Mac's brain from the couch.
• Your coding agents can use my knowledge and voice through a local MCP server and the m1k3 command-line tool.
• Siri and Shortcuts can ask me things and add to what I know.
• Private Cloud Compute, if you want it: off by default, one message at a time, with your consent.

Everything else runs on your Mac. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for model downloads, web search and the optional private cloud. Settings > Brains & downloads names every download; web search and the private cloud are switches in Settings > Privacy.
