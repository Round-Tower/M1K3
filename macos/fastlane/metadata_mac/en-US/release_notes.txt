NEW IN 1.4

• A local server for your other apps. Switch on the Local API in Settings > Server and any tool that speaks the OpenAI API (an editor, a script, a chat app) can use the brain you have loaded. It listens on this Mac only, is off by default and has its own key. You go first: a call waits while you are chatting, and ends if you start a turn.
• Apple's private cloud for your other apps, if you want it. Switch on "Also offer Private Cloud Compute" and a second model, cloud-m1k3, answers from Apple's Private Cloud Compute: a bigger brain for the jobs that need one. Your coding agents get the same through MCP, with a switch of their own. Both are off by default. Only what the app sends goes out, nothing of yours from me, and Settings counts every call.
• Settings > Server. The MCP server for your coding agents and the new Local API share one pane, each with its own switch and its own key.
• Mini answers the question. Mini had picked up a habit of answering ordinary coding questions with a line about not sharing its wiring. That is fixed.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Three brains: Mini (instant, on Apple Intelligence), Lil (4B, sees images) and Big (12B). Pick the one that fits your Mac.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: drop in notes and PDFs and I answer from them, on your Mac.
• I remember what matters to you, in a memory you can see, search and delete.
• Call memory: with your consent, I transcribe and summarise calls, encrypted on your Mac.
• Brain at Home: pair your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi and use your Mac's brain from the couch.
• Your coding agents can use my knowledge and voice through a local MCP server and the m1k3 command-line tool.
• Your other apps can use my brain through the Local API, on this Mac only.
• Siri and Shortcuts can ask me things and add to what I know.
• Private Cloud Compute, if you want it: off by default, one message at a time, with your consent.

Everything else runs on your Mac. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for model downloads, web search and the optional private cloud. Settings > Brains & downloads names every download; web search and the private cloud are switches in Settings > Privacy, and lending the private cloud to other apps is its own switch in Settings > Server.
