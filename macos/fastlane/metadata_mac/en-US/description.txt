An AI with a face that lives on your Mac. Talk to it, show it things, ask your PDFs — on-device, offline-ready, with no account.

My friends call me Mike. I run on your Mac, I remember what matters to you, and you can see everything I keep.

WHAT I DO
• Talk: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice (Kokoro).
• See: Lil and Big read images. Attach a photo, a screenshot or a whiteboard and ask about it; one tap keeps a private caption so you can ask again later.
• Know your stuff: drop in notes, PDFs and text files (.txt, .md, .csv, .log) and I answer from them, with on-device retrieval.
• Remember: a knowledge graph of what matters to you, which you can see, search and delete at any time.
• Speak your language: the app in English, German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese, and I reply in the language you write in.
• Brain at Home: pair your iPhone or iPad over your own network and use your Mac's big brain from the couch.

A FACE, NOT A TEXT BOX
• A pixel face that listens, thinks and talks back — or a friendly 3D companion, if you want one.

THREE BRAINS, PICK YOUR SIZE
• Mini: instant on Apple Intelligence Macs, a small download on others.
• Lil (4B): fast, sees images, and thinks a puzzle through step by step.
• Big (12B): the deep one, for Macs with 16 GB or more.
• Optimized for Apple Silicon (Metal and MLX), from MacBook Air to Mac Studio.

FOR DEVELOPERS
• A command-line client (m1k3) and a local MCP server, so your coding agents — Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Codex, Zed — can use my knowledge and voice.
• The Local API: any tool that speaks the OpenAI API can use the brain you have loaded. This Mac only, off by default, with its own key.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no analytics, no tracking. No data collected.
• Inference, search over your documents and voice all run on the Mac.
• The network is for downloading the brains and voices you choose, and for web search — on by default so answers can be current, one switch in Settings to turn it off.
• Optional: Apple's Private Cloud Compute as a brain, which you pick and consent to once. You can lend it to your other apps too: a separate switch, off by default, and Settings counts every call. Only what the app sends goes out.
• The source is published under a source-available licence, so you can check: github.com/Round-Tower/M1K3

Punk intelligence: yours, private, on your terms.
