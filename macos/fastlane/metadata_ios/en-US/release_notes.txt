NEW IN 1.6

• Dressed for the season. From 2 December I glow candy red and gold for Christmas and New Year, and through the year I dress up for Lunar New Year, Ramadan and Eid, St Patrick's Day and Nowruz. One switch in Settings turns the looks off.
• Code beside the chat. Ask me for a small web page and it opens by itself: in a sheet on iPhone, beside the chat on iPad. Tap Open on any code block to see that one. Pages are sealed: no scripts run and nothing loads from the internet.
• Share to M1K3 from any app. Share a link from Safari or some text from Notes, and it lands in my box, never sent. Open M1K3 to see it.
• Send me a file. AirDrop a text, code or JSON file to M1K3, or share it from Files, and it lands in the box, ready to ask about. A web page opens beside the chat too.
• If something arrives while I'm answering, I tell you it's waiting and put it in the box when I'm done.

IN CASE YOU MISSED IT (1.4 AND 1.5)

• Show me. Tap the eye at the top of a chat, point your camera and ask. I answer about what I see, on your device. In voice mode, just ask "what am I looking at?"
• I speak your language: the app is in German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese as well as English, and I reply in the language you write in.
• Read a text. Attach a .txt, .md, .csv or .log file from Files and ask about it.
• Leave while I answer. A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island shows when the answer is ready.
• Lil works through a multi-step problem in short steps, and I tell you in one plain line when a question needs real thought.
• In voice mode, the web page I quote pops up with the quote lit up.
• I say dates, money and numbers the way people do. Ask me to talk plainer and I will.
• Every control has a name for VoiceOver and Voice Control.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Show me things: point the camera or attach a photo, and I read it on your device.
• Chat with an on-device brain: Mini starts instantly on Apple Intelligence devices, and Lil runs fully offline on iPhones and iPads with 8 GB of memory.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: add PDFs and text files and I answer from them, on your device.
• I remember what matters to you, and you can see and search everything I've kept.
• A friendly 3D companion or a pixel face.
• Brain at Home: pair with your Mac over your own Wi-Fi and borrow its big brain from the couch.

By default everything runs on your device. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for brain and voice downloads, web search (one switch in Settings turns it off) and, only if you pick it, Private Cloud Compute.
