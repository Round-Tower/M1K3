NEW IN 1.5

• I speak your language. The app is now in German, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese as well as English, following your device's language, and I reply in the language you write in. In voice mode I still listen in English for now; an answer in another language is read by a voice that speaks it. A few privacy notes stay in English until a native speaker has checked them.
• Read a text. Attach a .txt, .md, .csv or .log file from Files and ask about it ("when does the warranty end?"). The answer sticks to the file and comes from your device. Ask more keeps asking about the same text; Keep this saves it to your documents.
• Leave while I answer. Lock your phone or go home during a long answer: a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island shows when it's ready, and a notification tells you. It never shows your question or the answer.
• Fixed: leaving the app while Lil was answering could close M1K3. It now stops where it was and picks up when you're back.
• Lil works it out. On a multi-step problem, a sum or a puzzle, Lil now works through it in short steps before answering: more right answers, and still the fast brain.
• I tell you when I'm thinking. When a question needs real thought, I say so in one plain line, instead of leaving you with a spinner.
• See what I'm quoting. In voice mode, when I quote a web page, tap the chip or say "show me that" and the page pops up with the quote lit up.
• I say numbers the way people do: dates, times, money, fractions, versions and "Henry VIII". And "I'm", "I'll" and "I've" sound right now.
• Plainer talk. The villain is the look, not the voice. Ask me to talk plainer and I will.
• Accessibility: every control has a name for VoiceOver and Voice Control, and dates and small print read larger.
• A clean start: a new chat opens without suggested questions.
• Fixed: an answer could stop at certain emoji.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Show me things: point the camera or attach a photo, and I read it on your device.
• Chat with an on-device brain: Mini starts instantly on Apple Intelligence devices, and Lil runs fully offline on iPhones and iPads with 8 GB of memory.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: add PDFs and text files and I answer from them, on your device.
• I remember what matters to you, and you can see and search everything I've kept.
• A friendly 3D companion or a pixel face.
• Brain at Home: pair with your Mac over your own Wi-Fi and borrow its big brain from the sofa.

Everything runs on your device. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for brain downloads and web search — one switch in Settings turns web search off.
