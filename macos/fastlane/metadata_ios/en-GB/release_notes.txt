IN CASE YOU MISSED 1.2

• Downloads that keep going. A brain download carries on when you leave the app, with its progress on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The bar never freezes at 0%, and a stall is named.
• Hear my answers. Turn on Speak answers and I read each reply aloud as it arrives, in the voice you chose. Small sounds mark answers and voice mode; Sound effects turns them off.
• Smoother thinking. Lil keeps its thinking to a set budget and answers straight after, so a hard question never runs away.
• I hear names better. In voice mode I fix a misheard name towards the people and places already in my memory.
• Better memory answers. Ask what you decided or told me and I look in your knowledge first, not the web.
• Memory capture off now means never. Turns you send with it off are never kept, even after a relaunch.
• A straight answer about privacy. Ask what leaves your device and I lead with the truth, and I decide when to search.

NEW TO M1K3? HERE'S WHAT I DO

• Chat with an on-device brain: Mini starts instantly on Apple Intelligence devices, and Lil runs fully offline on iPhones and iPads with 8 GB of memory.
• Talk to me: on-device speech recognition and a natural neural voice.
• Bring your own knowledge: add PDFs and text files and I answer from them, on your device.
• I remember what matters to you, and you can see and search everything I've kept.
• A friendly 3D companion or a pixel face.
• Brain at Home: pair with your Mac over your own Wi-Fi and borrow its big brain from the sofa.

Everything runs on your device. No account, no telemetry. The network is used for brain downloads and web search — one switch in Settings turns web search off.
